We teach machines to use cardiac imaging, multiomic signatures, and ECGs to uncover mechanisms of
cardiovascular disease and enable diagnosis where none was possible. The lab is part of the Division
of Cardiovascular Medicine at the University of Michigan, led by [Venk Murthy, MD, PhD](https://github.com/venkmurthy).

### Research

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vlmurthylab/.github/main/profile/dot-amber.png" width="12" height="12" alt=""> **Coronary vasomotor and microvascular dysfunction.** Quantifying impaired vasodilator function in
vessels below the resolution of angiography with cardiac PET. About half of patients referred for
stress testing have it despite normal perfusion imaging, and it predicts cardiac death.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vlmurthylab/.github/main/profile/dot-cyan.png" width="12" height="12" alt=""> **Multiomic biomarkers and mechanistic inference.** Metabolomic, proteomic, and genetic signatures
in longitudinal cohorts such as CARDIA, MESA, and the Framingham Heart Study, read for pathway as well as prediction.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vlmurthylab/.github/main/profile/dot-violet.png" width="12" height="12" alt=""> **AI and machine learning for imaging and risk.** Foundation models and deep learning that extend
quantitative capability to widely available tests like the 12-lead ECG.

### Code

| Repository | What it is |
|---|---|
| [**ecgflow**](https://github.com/vlmurthylab/ecgflow) | Self-supervised foundation transformer for ECG-based assessment of cardiac and coronary function |
| [pytorch-image-models-ecgflow](https://github.com/vlmurthylab/pytorch-image-models-ecgflow) | Fork of `timm` with 1D ECG input and self-supervised pretraining support, required by ECGFlow |
| [vmtools](https://github.com/vlmurthylab/vmtools) | R helpers for cardiometabolic epidemiology: PREVENT and PCE risk equations, insulin-resistance indices, GLI spirometry predictions, HMDB lookups |
| [murthylab-website](https://github.com/vlmurthylab/murthylab-website) | Source for [murthylab.org](https://murthylab.org): static HTML, publications generated from Zotero |
